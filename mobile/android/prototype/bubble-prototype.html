Phone bubble Prototype

Idle should say "tap to talk" at a glance, so each idle option is a mic. Talking options are the current desktop-pill animation plus three tries at doing better. Tap the bubble (in the phone or the big view) to talk, tap again to stop. The demo voice is fake; "Use my mic" drives it with your own voice. Idle never moves, on purpose: a bubble that always moves distracts and costs battery.
